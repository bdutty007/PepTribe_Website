Our Story

Born From a Desire for Something Better

Founded in May 2026, PepTribe was created from one simple belief: people deserve better than conflicting advice, marketing hype, and endless wellness trends.

Our founders experienced firsthand how difficult it was to separate credible science from misinformation. Instead of finding a trusted place to learn, they found fragmented conversations and confusing claims that often prioritized products over people.

That experience inspired them to build the community they wished had existed — a place where education, evidence, and meaningful conversations come together to help people make informed decisions with confidence.

🚀 Founded May 2026

Why We Exist

PepTribe exists to make wellness science more accessible through trusted education, thoughtful discussion, and a supportive community that values curiosity over hype.

Today, members from all backgrounds come together to explore research, share experiences, ask better questions, and grow toward healthier, more informed lives.

"We wanted to build the community we wished had existed when we were first trying to understand our own biology."
